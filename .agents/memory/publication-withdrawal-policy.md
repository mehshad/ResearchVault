---
name: Publication withdrawal policy
description: The approved terminal-exit rule for manuscripts in the publication workflow.
---

Do not offer or permit rejection for publications at any workflow stage. Linked authors may withdraw their own manuscripts, and that is the sole manuscript exit before publication.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected a separate publication-rejection workflow as unnecessary; author-controlled withdrawal covers the intended need.

**How to apply:** Do not add Outcome Office reject controls or transitions into `Rejected`. Preserve display and recovery handling for historical rejected records so existing data is not stranded.
