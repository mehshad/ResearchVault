---
name: Investigator eligibility
description: The product rule for staff who may serve in investigator-only roles.
---

Keep a staff member's primary job title intact when they also serve as an Investigator. Investigator-only roles accept existing Investigator and Staff Scientist titles plus an additional Investigator designation that only Management or administrators may change.

**Why:** Clinicians and other staff may lead research without giving up the job title that describes their main employment role, but staff must not be able to promote themselves into investigator authority.

**How to apply:** Any profile-creation path (including first-time registration) must block self-assignment through both the extra designation and legacy eligible titles. Every project-lead, PI, or investigator-only workflow must enforce the shared rule on the server, not only in selector UI.
