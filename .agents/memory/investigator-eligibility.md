---
name: Investigator eligibility
description: The product rule for staff who may serve in investigator-only roles.
---

Profile job title is self-editable and separate from the account access role assigned in User Management. Investigator-only roles accept existing Investigator and Staff Scientist titles plus an additional Investigator designation that only Management or administrators may change.

**Why:** Job title describes the staff profile and must not be confused with authorization. The separate account role controls application access, while the additional Investigator designation remains an administrator-controlled override.

**How to apply:** Own-profile edits and first-time registration may set job title but must reject direct self-assignment of the additional Investigator designation. Every project-lead, PI, or investigator-only workflow must enforce the shared eligibility rule on the server, not only in selector UI.
